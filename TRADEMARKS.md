## Bundled profile data

Bundled scanner-profile files are derived from measured scan samples and are named after the film stock and scanner model they were measured from, solely to identify those inputs. They are not, and do not claim to be, the output of, or certified by, any manufacturer.

## GrainMend defect removal

**GrainMend** is the product name for negaflow's dust-and-scratch removal feature. Its RGB and infrared paths are independent implementations based on published imaging principles. It is not "Digital ICE", "iSRD", "SRDx", "FARE", or any other branded dust-and-scratch system, and does not claim equivalence to hardware infrared cleaning.
